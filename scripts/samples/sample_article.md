# Understanding High-Yield Savings and Certificates of Deposit: A Smart Saver's Guide

When building your personal emergency fund or saving for a major milestone, choosing the right deposit account can significantly accelerate your interest earnings while keeping your principal secure. Two of the most popular vehicles offered by our bank are High-Yield Savings Accounts and Certificates of Deposit (CDs).

## 1. What is a High-Yield Savings Account?

Unlike traditional checking accounts that earn nominal interest, a High-Yield Savings Account offers an elevated Annual Percentage Yield (APY), currently up to 4.50%. Your funds remain liquid, meaning you can initiate electronic funds transfers (EFT) or Automated Clearing House (ACH) withdrawals at any time without paying an early redemption penalty.

Every deposit account with our institution is backed by the full faith and credit of the United States government through the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC), insuring your deposits up to $250,000 per depositor, per account ownership category.

## 2. Certificates of Deposit (CDs) for Guaranteed Returns

If you have idle cash that you do not anticipate needing for six to sixty months, a Certificate of Deposit locks in a fixed rate of return. For example, our 12-month fixed CD currently offers an APY of 5.15% on balances exceeding $10,000. 

While CDs protect you from interest rate fluctuations in the broader bond market, withdrawing principal before the scheduled maturity date will incur an early withdrawal penalty.

## 3. Account Opening and Verification

Opening an account on our website or mobile banking app takes under five minutes. In accordance with federal Know Your Customer (KYC) and Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) regulations, please have a government-issued photo ID and your Social Security Number ready during onboarding.
